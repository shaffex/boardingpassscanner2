User Guide

Boarding Pass Scanner — everything you need for every flight

Boarding Pass Scanner turns the barcode on your boarding pass into a clean, readable pass you can keep, customise, add to Apple Wallet and track live right up to departure. Here's how to get the most out of it.

✈️ Adding a boarding pass

From the Home tab you have three quick ways to add a pass:

1

Scan with the camera

Tap Scan Boarding Pass and point your camera at the barcode (PDF417, Aztec or QR). It's detected automatically — no need to press a shutter. Grant camera access the first time you scan.

2

Import from Photos

Tap Import Boarding Pass to pick a screenshot or photo of a boarding pass from your photo library. The barcode is read directly from the image on your device.

3

Paste from the clipboard

Copied a barcode string or screenshot from an airline email or app? Tap Paste Boarding Pass and we'll detect it from your clipboard.

Boarding Pass Scanner reads the standard IATA BCBP data encoded in the barcode — passenger name, flight number, origin and destination, seat, sequence number and more.

🎫 My Passes

Every pass you add appears in the My Passes tab. From here you can:

  • Browse all your saved boarding passes.
  • Tap any pass to open its full details.
  • Swipe to delete a pass you no longer need.

Your next upcoming flight is also surfaced at the top of the Home tab under Next Flight, so it's always one tap away.

🔎 Pass details & customisation

Open a pass to see the live barcode plus all of its decoded fields. You can:

  • Show the scannable barcode full-screen at the gate.
  • View the decoded IATA BCBP fields for the flight.
  • Customise pass fields — choose which details appear and personalise the look of your pass.

Note: pass customisation is disabled while live flight tracking is active for that pass (see below).

📍 Live flight tracking

This is one of the most useful features in the app. When you enable Flight Tracking on a pass, Boarding Pass Scanner creates a Live Activity for supported flights so you can follow your flight directly from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island — without even opening the app.

How to turn it on

  1. Open a boarding pass from My Passes or Next Flight.
  2. Find the Flight Tracking panel.
  3. Switch on Enable flight tracking.

What you get

  • A Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island for supported flights.
  • Up-to-date flight information at a glance as your departure approaches.
  • Your boarding pass can be shown automatically on the Lock Screen around departure time.

Lock Screen timing

The app tries to detect your departure time from the flight number automatically. You can override it in the Lock Screen Timing panel — this controls when the boarding pass is shown automatically on your Lock Screen.

Live tracking works for supported flights. While tracking is active for a pass, customisation of that pass is paused so the live data stays accurate.

📲 Add to Apple Wallet

From a pass's detail screen, tap Add to Apple Wallet to create a native Wallet pass for your flight. Once it's in Wallet, iOS can surface it on your Lock Screen near departure, and you can present it at the gate just like an airline's own pass.

🛠️ Tools

The Tools tab gives you a few extra capabilities:

  • Data Library — built-in airline and airport databases used to turn codes into friendly names. You can check for and download data updates here.
  • Import / export passes — back up your boarding passes or move them between devices.
  • Report incorrect airline or airport data so we can fix it.

⚙️ Settings

  • Show barcode text — display the raw barcode string beneath the code.
  • Show seat number on barcode — keep your seat visible on the scannable view.
  • About — app version, links to rate the app, the Privacy Policy and Contact Us.

🔒 Your privacy

Your boarding passes stay on your device. We don't collect or store the contents of your passes. The camera processes barcodes on-device, and imported images are read locally. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

💬 Need more help?

Can't scan a pass, or spotted incorrect flight data? We're happy to help.

  • Make sure the whole barcode is in frame and well lit when scanning.
  • If a screenshot won't import, try a clearer, uncropped image.
  • Still stuck? Email us at support@shaffex.com or use Contact Us in Settings.
